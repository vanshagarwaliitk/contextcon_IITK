P0: Mission-Critical Data (The Core Engine)
These are the foundational inputs your agent must successfully retrieve to generate a baseline analysis.

Company Identity & Firmographic Baselines: The agent must accurately resolve the target company via its name and domain to ensure an exact entity match. It must extract baseline firmographics, including official industry tags, headquarters location, and overall employee headcount trends (growth vs. shrinkage).

Hiring Velocity & Job Signals: Job postings are the strongest early indicator of strategic shifts. The system must scrape recent job data to capture the specific skills requested, departmental focus (e.g., "Hardware" vs. "Software"), hiring volume, and the geographic locations of new roles.

P1: High-Value Intelligence (The Multipliers)
These inputs act as corroborating evidence. Combining P1 data with P0 data drastically increases the confidence score of the agent's hypotheses.

Leadership & Talent Movement: Utilize person-search endpoints to track high-impact personnel changes. The system should monitor founder activity, newly hired executives, board member appointments, and senior engineers poached from major competitors (e.g., hiring a new "VP of Machine Learning").

Digital Footprint & Web Evidence: Track public-facing changes to the company's web presence. This includes updates to pricing tiers, the launch of new product landing pages, explicit product announcements, and recent press coverage.
